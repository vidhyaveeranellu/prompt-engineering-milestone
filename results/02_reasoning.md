
# Experiment 2: Direct vs Step-by-Step Prompting

## Problem


A shopkeeper buys 5 notebooks for ₹40 each.
He sells each notebook for ₹55 each.
He spends ₹25 on transportation.

Calculate his total profit.


## Direct prompt


Solve this problem and give only the final profit.


A shopkeeper buys 5 notebooks for ₹40 each.
He sells each notebook for ₹55 each.
He spends ₹25 on transportation.

Calculate his total profit.



## Actual direct output

₹50

## Step-by-step prompt


Solve the following mathematical problem.

Show the necessary calculation steps clearly:
1. Calculate total purchase cost.
2. Calculate total selling price.
3. Include transportation cost.
4. Calculate final profit.

Do not skip any calculation.

Problem:

A shopkeeper buys 5 notebooks for ₹40 each.
He sells each notebook for ₹55 each.
He spends ₹25 on transportation.

Calculate his total profit.



## Actual step-by-step output

Here are the step-by-step calculations to find the total profit:

### **Step 1: Calculate total purchase cost**
* Number of notebooks bought = 5
* Cost of 1 notebook = ₹40

$$\text{Total Purchase Cost} = 5 \times 40 = \text{₹200}$$

---

### **Step 2: Calculate total selling price**
* Number of notebooks sold = 5
* Selling price of 1 notebook = ₹55

$$\text{Total Selling Price} = 5 \times 55 = \text{₹275}$$

---

### **Step 3: Include transportation cost**
* Transportation cost = ₹25

$$\text{Total Effective Cost} = \text{Total Purchase Cost} + \text{Transportation Cost}$$
$$\text{Total Effective Cost} = 200 + 25 = \text{₹225}$$

---

### **Step 4: Calculate final profit**
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Total Selling Price} - \text{Total Effective Cost}$$
$$\text{Profit} = 275 - 225 = \text{₹50}$$

---

### **Final Answer:**
The shopkeeper's total profit is **₹50**.

## Independently calculated answer

Purchase cost = 5 × 40 = ₹200

Selling price = 5 × 55 = ₹275

Total cost = 200 + 25 = ₹225

Profit = 275 - 225 = ₹50

Expected profit = ₹50
